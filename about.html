About Us

A club built on good company and a good green.

Bowls Hornby is a lawn bowls club on the Hornby Domain in Christchurch, open to players of every level.

Our Home

Right on the Hornby Domain

Our clubhouse and green sit on the Hornby Domain at 521 Main South Road, with easy parking and a welcoming clubhouse for a cuppa after a game. It's a relaxed, community-minded green — the kind of place where regulars know your name by your second visit.

What to Expect

Bowls for every kind of player

Social bowls

Casual, friendly games for players who want to enjoy an afternoon on the green without the pressure of competition.

Club competitions

For members who want to test their skills, we run internal competitions throughout the season — see our coach for current fixtures.

Coaching & guidance

New players are paired with an experienced club member to learn the basics — grip, delivery, and reading the green.

Every player started as a beginner. If you're curious about lawn bowls but have never picked up a bowl before, that's exactly who this club is for. Give us a call and come have a go — see how to join.
